# Notice

## Where this came from

The scenario (McContext Burger, the Double Stack launch, Marcus J. Williams and his eight questions) and the recorded answers in `data/marcus.js` follow a public demo published by Atlan at
https://grand-belekoy-2d77e8.netlify.app/agents.html. They are reproduced here for an internal demonstration, with the context layer shown as Regenesys AI. McContext Burger is fictional.

Everything else was written for this project: the page and its styles, the two logos, the generated warehouse, the context repository, the live engine, the analyses for all six people, and the tools.

The two logos in `assets/img/` are placeholders. Replace them with your own artwork before showing this outside your organisation, and check with the owner of the original demo before publishing a version that reuses its scenario or wording.
