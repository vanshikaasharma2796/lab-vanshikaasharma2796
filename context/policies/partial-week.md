policy: partial-week
name: Partial weeks
owner: Central Reporting
applies_to: [this week, weekly totals, week on week]
---

A reporting week with fewer than seven days of data is partial.

- Say how many days it covers whenever its figures are quoted.
- Averages may be reported. Totals may not be compared with a full week.
- For revenue, compare per-day figures across the same number of days.
