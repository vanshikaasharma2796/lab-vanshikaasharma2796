name: Who can see what
owner: Franchise Relations
applies_to: [every answer]

- A franchise operator sees their own stores. "My stores" is their portfolio and nothing else.
- Corporate roles (Ads, Store Ops, Customer Support, Supply Chain, analysts) see every store.
- When a corporate user names an operator, a store or a region, the answer is scoped to it.
- Dollar figures shown to an operator cover that operator's stores only.
