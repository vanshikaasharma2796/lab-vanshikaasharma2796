# Staffing playbook: unfilled shifts

Staffing is measured as shift fill rate: worked crew hours over scheduled crew hours. Headcount does not move when a shift is missed, so never use it to judge staffing.

## Warning signs

Fill rate below 92% for two weeks running, or a slide of more than five points since the start of the quarter. Prep time creeps up first; window time follows.

## What the operator does

Re-post open shifts to the cross-store pool, move the strongest crew to the pre-rush, and stop approving swaps that leave the 10am and 4pm windows short.

## When corporate steps in

If fill rate keeps falling for four weeks, the franchise business partner schedules a staffing review with the operator and People.
