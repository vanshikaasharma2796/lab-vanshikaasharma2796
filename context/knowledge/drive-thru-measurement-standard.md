# Drive-thru measurement standard

Drive-thru time is measured gate to window by the drive-thru timer system. It is the sum of order time, prep time and window time.

## Which number counts

The certified figure is the average on the timing mart. The service-seconds column in the POS feed measures something else and must not be quoted as drive-thru time.

## Benchmarks

Single-lane stores are held to 165 sec and dual-lane stores to 185 sec. A blended benchmark weights the two by store count.

## Reporting weeks

Weeks run Monday to Sunday. A week with fewer than seven days of data is partial: say how many days whenever its figures are quoted.
