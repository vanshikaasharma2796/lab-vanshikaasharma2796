# Honey Heat Chicken launch readiness checklist

Honey Heat Chicken is the next limited-time offer, planned for June 4. Like the Double Stack it is a two-step item: fry, then a sauce toss.

## A kitchen is ready when

It has parallel staging, or its window time is under 45 sec on the current menu. Kitchens that are neither should not get the item on day one.

## Four weeks out

Supply Chain confirms sauce cover and fry-oil stock by region. Store Ops confirms which single-lane kitchens are still exposed.

## Two weeks out

Marketing holds paid media in regions where exposed kitchens are still over the window limit. Care desk gets the wait-time script.

## Launch week

Pre-stage from day one in the 10am and 4pm pre-rush. Review window time after five trading days.
