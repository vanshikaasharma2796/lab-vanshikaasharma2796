# How to pre-stage a two-step item

Pre-staging means cooking and holding the components of a two-step item just before the rush, so the second step at the window is an assembly, not a cook. It is step 1 of the ops playbook and needs no new equipment.

## When to pre-stage

Pre-stage in the two pre-rush windows: 10am ahead of lunch and 4pm ahead of dinner. Start on the next trading day after the decision; there is nothing to order and nothing to install.

## How to do it for the Double Stack

Cook the beef stacks in the pre-rush and hold them in the heated holding pans. Hold sauce-layer components in the cold well next to the assembly board. At the window the crew assembles: stack, sauce layer, wrap. Discard held stacks that pass the holding time on the pan label.

## How to tell it is working

Window time is the measure, not total drive-thru time. A store is working when window time is below 45 sec at lunch. Review after five trading days. If window time stays above 50 sec, move one crew member forward into the pre-rush, then escalate for a parallel prep station.

## Common mistakes

Pre-staging only at lunch and not at 4pm. Holding sauce warm, which makes it run. Reading POS service seconds instead of the drive-thru timer.
