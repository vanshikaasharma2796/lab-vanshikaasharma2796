# Decision record: allocating PPS-2 retrofit kits

Decided on May 12 by Supply Chain with Store Ops. The PPS-2 retrofit kit turns an exposed single-lane kitchen into one with parallel staging, in one overnight install.

## Decision

Kits go to the stores with the most weekly revenue at risk first, not first come, first served. Stock on hand ships from the nearest distribution centre the next day; anything beyond stock waits for the supplier lead time.

## Why

Linda Hartmann's four retrofitted Midwest kitchens, fitted in February as a pilot, held window time through the launch while exposed single-lane kitchens did not. That is the evidence the kit works.

## Constraints

Three install crews, one install per crew per night. Supplier lead time and installed cost are on the certified distribution-centre stock table; quote them from there, not from this page.

## Review

Revisit the order once a week while the Double Stack is on the menu.
