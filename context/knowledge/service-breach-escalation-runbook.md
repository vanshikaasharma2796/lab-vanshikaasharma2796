# Runbook: escalating a service breach

A group of stores is in a service breach when it averages more than 30 sec over its drive-thru benchmark, or serves fewer than half its orders inside benchmark.

## First 24 hours

The operator confirms the breach on the certified timing table, not the POS. The area coach checks the three usual causes in order: a launch or menu change, staffing, then equipment.

## Escalation path

Open a Store Ops ticket in ServiceNow with the stores, the seconds over benchmark and the cause found. If window time stays above 50 sec after five trading days of the ops playbook, Store Ops escalates for a parallel prep station.

## Corporate ops review

Five or more stores at 30 sec or more over benchmark, or stores more than 5 sec over for four weeks running, put the operator on the corporate intervention list. Franchise Relations owns that call.
