# Double Stack launch FAQ

The McContext Double Stack launched nationally on May 7 as a limited-time offer. It is a two-step item: the beef stack is cooked first, then the Stack Sauce layer is added.

## Why is it slower at some stores?

Single-lane kitchens without parallel staging do both steps on one line, so window time rises when the Double Stack mix climbs. Dual-lane stores and retrofitted single-lane kitchens stage the two steps in parallel and barely move.

## What is the planned mix?

The launch plan assumed the Double Stack would be 22% of drive-thru orders. Stack Sauce was allocated to stores on that plan mix.

## What did the field get?

An ops alert went out on May 9 for the Southeast: pre-stage components in the 10am and 4pm pre-rush. The full ops playbook applies to every region.

## Who answers questions?

Store Ops owns the playbook, Supply Chain owns sauce and kits, Marketing owns the promo calendar.
