# Care desk guide: wait-time complaints

A wait-time complaint is a ticket whose certified category is wait_time, whatever words the customer used.

## What agents say

Apologise for the wait, say the store team knows and is working on it, and offer a $5 voucher. Never promise a fix date that ops has not confirmed, and never blame the store team.

## Handling time

Plan on 12 minutes per wait-time ticket including the follow-up note.

## When to escalate to ops

Escalate a store with four or more wait-time tickets in a week, or one running more than 30 sec over its benchmark. Open the escalation in ServiceNow and copy the area coach.

## Personal data

Do not paste customer emails or phone numbers into Slack or Teams. Reference the ticket number instead.
