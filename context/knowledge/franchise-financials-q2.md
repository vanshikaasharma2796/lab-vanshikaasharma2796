# Franchise financials: Q2 royalty and margin outlook

Corporate only. Royalty income and operator margin outlook for the quarter, by region.

## Royalty outlook

Royalty is charged on net sales. The Double Stack adds net sales in every region, but combo discounting during the launch window trims the royalty base.

## Operator margins

Operators with many exposed single-lane kitchens carry the most risk this quarter: drive-thru revenue at risk falls straight to their margin. Finance tracks those operators monthly with Franchise Relations.

## Disclosure

Do not share operator-level figures with other operators. Each operator sees only their own portfolio.
