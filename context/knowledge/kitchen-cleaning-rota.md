# Kitchen equipment cleaning rota

Every kitchen follows the same cleaning rota for its equipment, so that no cleaning task lands in the lunch or dinner rush.

## Daily

Filter the fryer oil after the lunch rush and again at close. Scrape the grill plates between the lunch and dinner rush, never during it. Empty and wipe the heated holding pans at close.

## Weekly

Boil out one fryer vat each Tuesday after close. Descale the beverage station on Sunday night. The shift manager signs the rota sheet when each task is done.

## Who to call

If a fryer or grill fails its daily check, log a ticket with the equipment desk and move the item to the other fryer or grill until it is fixed.
