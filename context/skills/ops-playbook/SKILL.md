# Ops playbook for two-step items

Use when window time is the bottleneck and the menu change log shows a two-step item.

1. **Pre-stage** the item's components in the 10am and 4pm pre-rush. Free, starts the next day.
2. **Move one crew member forward** into those pre-rush slots.
3. **Fit a parallel prep station.** The structural fix. It brings a single-lane kitchen to the level of the ones that already have one.

Review after five trading days. Window time under 45 seconds: it is working. Still above 50: escalate to step 3.

Never recommend pulling the item before steps 1 and 2 have been tried.
