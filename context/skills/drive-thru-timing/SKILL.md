# Drive-thru timing

Drive-thru time = order time + prep time + window time.

- **Order** rises when the order point is short of people or the menu is confusing.
- **Prep** rises when the kitchen is short of hands or an item takes longer to cook.
- **Window** rises when finished orders queue at the handoff. A two-step item with nowhere to be assembled shows up here.

The bottleneck is the segment with the largest change since the baseline, not the largest segment.
