# Stock allocation

Stores should hold four days of cover.

Stock is allocated from a planning assumption. The Double Stack launch allocation assumed it would be 22% of drive-thru orders in every region. When actual usage runs ahead of plan in one region and behind in another, move stock between distribution centres rather than waiting for the supplier.

A donor centre must be left with at least nine days of its own region's usage. Transfers take two days.
