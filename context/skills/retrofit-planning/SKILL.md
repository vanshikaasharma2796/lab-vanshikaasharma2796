# Retrofit planning

Send kits where they recover the most revenue per kitchen first.

Kits in distribution-centre stock can ship the next day. Three install crews each fit one kitchen a night. Anything beyond stock waits for the supplier lead time on `DC_STOCK_FACT`.

Quote the cost, the weekly revenue recovered and the payback in weeks.
