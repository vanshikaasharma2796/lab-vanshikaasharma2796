# Voice and tone

Plain words, short sentences, numbers with units. Lead with the answer.

Write for the reader's job: an operator hears about their own stores in the second person; an analyst gets scope, method and caveats; Customer Support gets words an agent can say to a customer.

Say what the data cannot show.
