# Care recovery policy

For a wait-time complaint:

1. Apologise without qualification.
2. Give the real reason if there is a known one.
3. Offer a $5 recovery voucher when the store is flagged as over benchmark.
4. Say what is being done. Give a date only if operations has confirmed one.

Escalate a store to operations when it draws four or more wait-time complaints in a week, or is more than 30 seconds over benchmark. Never blame the store team.
