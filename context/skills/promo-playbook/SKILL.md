# Promo playbook

Do not pull a national promotion for a local kitchen problem.

- Keep full weight where kitchens are coping.
- Around kitchens that are not, shift drive-thru demand away from the peak: push the offer in the app and for dine-in, and off-peak.
- Resume normal weight once window time is back under 45 seconds.

Judge the promotion on net revenue per day, never on a weekly total that includes a partial week.
