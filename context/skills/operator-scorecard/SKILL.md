# Operator scorecard

Judge an operator on like-for-like stores. Never rank operators on a plain average: the lane mix decides it.

- If their exposed single-lane stores are over benchmark but their dual-lane stores are fine, the problem is the kitchen, not the operator. They need capital.
- If their stores were already drifting before the event, look at prep time and shift fill rate. They need an operations review.

The intervention trigger is in the glossary. Operators with fewer stores over than the trigger go on a watch list.
