# Monday ops digest

Lead with at most three findings, ranked by dollars at stake and by whether leadership has to decide something.

Each finding has one number, one decision and one caveat. Put anything that is only "worth knowing" below the fold.

Open with the segment view when an average hides a split. Flag a partial week wherever its figures appear.
