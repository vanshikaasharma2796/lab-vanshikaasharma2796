Drive-thru revenue a store is likely to lose because cars leave a slow line.

The agreed rule: every 30 seconds over benchmark costs about 8% of drive-thru orders, valued at the store's average ticket. Stores within 5 seconds of benchmark count as zero.

Quote it as a range. The top of the range weights the loss towards peak hours.
